We've got a naming update, and we want to explain it plainly instead of just quietly changing logos on you.

Up until now, we've used "AutoButler" for pretty much everything — the company, the app, the whole idea. That worked fine when we were small, but it started to get confusing. AutoButler is the company: the LLC, the "we" that's building this, the thing that answers to you as customers instead of to investors. It was never really supposed to be the name of a piece of software. So we're fixing that.

**The product itself is now called Quark.**

AutoButler is still AutoButler — same company, same mission, same no-subscription, no-venture-capital, bootstrapped approach we've talked about from day one. That part hasn't moved an inch. What's changed is that the thing you actually install, open, and put your files and photos into now has its own name, separate from the company that makes it.

[Brandon: this is the part worth writing in your own words — why "Quark" specifically. If it's the physics angle, something like: we liked that a quark is one of the smallest, most fundamental building blocks of matter — plain, foundational, not flashy. That fit what we wanted this thing to feel like: the basic, dependable unit your files and photos sit on, nothing more complicated than it needs to be.]

Practically, here's what this means for you:

- Nothing about your setup, your data, or how the product works has changed. This is a name change, not a product change.
- Going forward, you'll see "Quark" in the app, on the website, and in anything we write. If you see "AutoButler" mentioned, that's us talking about the company behind Quark, not a different product.
- Your bookmarks, your existing installs, and your data are unaffected.

We thought about just letting this happen quietly, but that's not really how we like to do things around here. You've been paying attention to what we're building, so you deserve to know why the name on the box changed. Thanks for sticking with us through it.
